How it works

A binaural beat arises when each ear hears its own tone: from two slightly different tones you hear a slow swell and fade.

  1. Pick a preset

    More than 30 presets, sorted by occasion: sleep, focus, meditation, energy, creativity, sports, relaxation, Solfeggio and isochronic tones.

  2. Headphones on, start

    Binaural beats need headphones. Isochronic tones – a single tone that pulses rhythmically – also work through the speaker. Playback continues in the background.

  3. Adjust and take notes

    In the phase editor you set frequency, length, modulation and background of every phase. Afterwards you rate the session in the journal.

To be honest

Whether binaural beats measurably improve sleep or concentration is not scientifically settled; studies reach different conclusions. That’s why DeepWave promises no effect. It generates the tones cleanly and lets you note in the journal what works for you. DeepWave is not a medical device and does not replace medical treatment.

Who is it for?

Falling asleep

A calm preset, rain or ocean from the mixer on top, and the sleep timer fades everything out at the end. With the sound alarm, a ramp that starts quietly wakes you in the morning instead of a shrill beep.

Working and studying

Focus presets and a Pomodoro preset as an even backdrop for desk time – offline, with no ads breaking in.

Meditation and breathing

The breathing guide runs along with the session. The rhythm track gives an audible beat for walking meditation or steady breathing.

Tinkerers

Build sessions from your own phases, share them as a link or take them along as WAV files. DeepWave is open source (GPL-3.0) – the source code is on GitHub.

FAQ

Do I need headphones?

For binaural beats, yes: the beat only arises when each ear hears its own tone. Isochronic tones, the ambient mixer and the sound alarm also work through the speaker.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. All tones, and the mixer sounds too, are generated in real time on your device. Only purchases go through Google Play.

Do binaural beats work?

That is not scientifically settled; studies reach different conclusions. DeepWave promises no effect, is not a medical device and does not replace medical diagnosis or treatment. If you have health concerns, talk to a doctor.

Is DeepWave available for iPhone?

No, DeepWave is only available for Android.

Does the app collect data?

No account, no ads, no trackers. Journal, custom presets and settings stay on your device. The details are in the privacy policy.

I got a new phone. Are my purchases gone?

No. Purchases are tied to your Google account and apply to all your devices. If they don’t show up right away, “Restore purchase” brings them back.

Which languages does the app support?

English and German.
