How it works

No fighting, no rush. The story grows out of what you do by the fire.

  1. Feed the fire

    At first there is only you, Mara, three logs, and an ember hardly bigger than a walnut. Keep it alive.

  2. Assign your people

    Whoever arrives helps build. You send people to cut wood, keep watch, tend the kiln, or explore the paths.

  3. Read on

    Day by day the settlement grows—and the story tells itself line by line until a chapter ends.

What awaits you

A settlement grows around the embers

Gather wood, build cottages, burn charcoal in the kiln. An abandoned clearing becomes a village.

A story in six chapters

Mara, the charcoal burner, counts under her breath on her fingers. Hannes never turns back before he has seen where he was going. The Old Man speaks only in his grandfather’s sayings. And little Fenja hears the trees breathe. Your decisions lead to one of three endings.

Calm, but not without tension

Rain, frost, a kiln that draws wrong: every danger announces itself, and you have a few nights to prepare. None of it is left to chance. If the fire does go out, the game isn’t over.

While you’re away

The watch keeps the fire and your people keep gathering. After a longer break, a card tells you what happened. No notifications calling you back.

Tally stick and journal

One notch per chapter—tap it to reread. The journal keeps every line of the story, even across a fresh start.

Made for everyone

“Calm Pace”: everything runs at half speed, and the fire never goes out completely. Plus large text, light and dark themes, and reduced motion.

FAQ

When will the game be released?

The Ninth Notch is in testing. When it comes out on Google Play, the link will be on this page.

Do I need an internet connection?

No, the whole story is inside the app. Only for the purchase does the Google Play library connect to Google Play.

Is there an iPhone version?

No, so far it’s Android only.

Do I have to keep checking in?

No. The game sends no notifications. While you’re away, the watch keeps the fire, and when you return, a card tells you what happened.

Does the app collect data?

No. No account, no ads, no trackers; the app itself sends nothing. Your progress and journal stay on your device. The details are in the privacy policy (German).

I got a new phone. Is my purchase gone?

No. Your purchase is tied to your Google account. On the new device, “Restore purchase” brings it back.

Which languages are available?

English and German. The game follows your device’s language.

I found a mistake. Where do I report it?

Write to us at kontakt@wunderkammer-apps.de—ideally with the chapter and day it happened.
