How it works

Get a text, pick a speed, look

  1. Get a text

    Share an article from another app, paste a URL, open a text file or choose one of the 24 public-domain classics.

  2. Pick a speed

    The speed slider shows the zones from research. A short baseline test measures your natural pace and recommends a starting speed; a gentle ramp lets you start slower.

  3. Read

    A swipe to the right replays the sentence – re-reading is part of understanding. Prefer context? Flow mode highlights the current word within the whole paragraph.

up to ≈ 350words per minute: comprehension stays intact.
from 400words per minute: comprehension measurably drops.
250words per minute: the app’s default.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Will I read three times faster?

No, and Wortgenau doesn’t promise that. Studies show that comprehension stays intact up to about 350 words per minute and measurably drops above 400. Wortgenau shows you these zones right on the speed slider.

Do I need an internet connection?

Only to fetch an article from a URL and to download a classic from Project Gutenberg the first time you open it. After that the text is on your device and you read offline.

Which files can I open?

Free: shared text, text from the clipboard, articles via URL and TXT files. With the full version also EPUB, PDF, DOCX, FB2 and HTML.

Is Wortgenau available for iPhone?

No, Wortgenau is only available for Android.

Does the app collect data?

No ads, no account, no data collection. Your library stays on your device. The details are in the privacy policy (German).

I got a new phone. Is my purchase gone?

No. The purchase is tied to your Google account; “Restore purchase” brings it back on the new device. With the full version you can also take your library along as a backup file.

Which languages does the app support?

The interface is available in English and German, and so are the classics.
