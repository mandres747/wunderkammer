What they all have in common

the small print, written large

  • No ads

    No banners, no videos, no pop-ups. All you see is the app.

  • Your data stays with you

    No account, no trackers. What you play, read or learn stays on your device.

  • Start free, pay once

    Every app can be tried for free. Anything extra is a one-time purchase – no subscription.

  • No internet needed

    The apps work offline. Only a purchase needs a connection for a moment.

  • Each with its own face

    Its own colours, its own typeface, its own motif – no two apps look alike.

  • Carefully checked

    Game rules with sources, learning content based on the curriculum, every text proofread.
